# ADR-0009: Game audio (mix, footsteps, vehicles, sirens)

*French version: [adr-0009-audio-mix.fr.md](adr-0009-audio-mix.fr.md)*

## Status

Proposed on 2026-11-10. Code written in the cloud, checked outside Unreal by
CI (footstep rules, sirens, squeal, generated banks); still to compile and
measure on the Mac.

## Date

2026-11-10

## Decision Makers

nic0lasm (project lead)

## Summary

Audio becomes a continuous workstream (roadmap v3, systems inventory line
"Audio"), not a pass at the end. The mix and every rule live in C++ and in
`Config/DefaultGame.ini`; the sounds themselves stay out of Git, generated by
our own scripts first, then replaced by recordings (Sonniss GDC bundle, CC0)
folder by folder. Nothing in the other systems' classes or in the map changes:
a world subsystem gives sounds to what appears in the world.

## Decisions

1. **Mix built in code** (`UGTPAudioMix`, one per game instance): one sound
   class per category (Master > Effects > Footsteps, Vehicles, Impacts,
   Sirens; Master > Ambience, Music, Voice, Interface), a user mix holding the
   category volumes (for the future options menu), a driving mix (street
   ambience at 60 % and low-passed at 6 kHz while the player drives),
   concurrency groups and attenuation presets per category. No binary mix
   asset to merge, every value is in `[/Script/GranTheftPraline.GTPAudioSettings]`.
2. **Banks found by folder**, not by reference:
   `/Game/Sound/SFX/Footsteps/<Surface>/`, `Land/<Surface>/`,
   `Impacts/<Kind>/<Light|Medium|Heavy>/`, `Impacts/Glass/`. Dropping recorded
   WAVs into a folder replaces the generated ones without code.
3. **Footsteps from the foot bones**, not from animation notifies: works with
   GASP motion matching and with the crowd's walk cycles. Surface: physical
   material if one is set, else keywords in the material name, else, on the
   city ground (one material driven by a mask), a 50 cm grid made from the
   same mask (`tools/audio/surface_map.py`: asphalt vs stone today). Rule:
   **the ground sounds like it looks**; gravel at Bellecour or grass arrive
   with the ground material that paints them (`--areas`, `--vegetation`).
4. **Procedural sounds where the sound follows the game continuously**: the
   engine (ADR-0003), the tyre squeal (sideways slide and Chaos wheel skid)
   and the siren. French police « deux tons » la 435 Hz / ré 580 Hz, 0.55 s
   each, plus a chase yelp; Doppler shift computed in the oscillator.
5. **One-shot banks generated first** (`tools/audio/make_sfx.py`: 8 surfaces
   × 8 steps and 3 landings, 5 impact kinds × 3 tiers × 3, glass), original
   and MIT, so the game has sound before any download.
6. **Kill switch**: `-Audio=0` (or `-NoGameAudio`) adds nothing of this ADR,
   for A/B measurements; a `[Audio]` line states it at start-up.
   `-AudioStats` writes `[Audio] steps=… impacts=… sirens_on=…` every second
   for the soak tests.

## Interfaces for the other systems

| System | What it does | Change on its side |
|---|---|---|
| Player, pedestrians (ADR-0005) | `UGTPFootstepComponent` added to every `ACharacter` and `AGTPPedestrian` | none (hidden or attached = silent) |
| Vehicles, traffic (ADR-0003) | `UGTPVehicleAudioComponent` (impacts, squeal) on every `AWheeledVehiclePawn`; engine sound put in the Vehicles class | none |
| Police (ADR-0004) | `UGTPSirenComponent::SetSiren(Car, EGTPSirenMode::TwoTone / Yelp / Off)` | one line where the light bar switches |
| Options menu (later) | `UGTPAudioMix::SetCategoryVolume(Category, Volume)` | — |

## Performance budget (M1, agreed with the optimisation workstream)

0.3 ms of game thread, 10 % of one core for the audio thread, 0 ms GPU.
Voices: 10 steps, 6 impacts, 3 sirens, 4 squeals at most, the farthest then
the oldest stopped; a global cap of 48 voices is the optimisation
workstream's call (`AudioMaxChannels`). Steps are not looked for beyond 25 m,
the squeal not computed beyond 60 m (20 Hz). If the game thread goes over,
the ground probes per walker go down first.

## Consequences

- Positive: sound for every system from 0.3 on, no binary asset in Git, a
  single folder convention for generated and recorded sounds, measurable on
  and off.
- Negative: synthesised one-shots are a working base, not GTA quality:
  recordings must replace the footsteps and crashes before 1.0. The surface
  grid only knows the focus street; other places fall back to material
  names and to stone.
- Risks: sound classes created at run time must register with the audio
  device (checked on the Mac); foot-bone heights need one calibration pass
  (`gtp.Audio.DebugFeet 1`); GASP may already play its own foley steps (to
  check, and mute one of the two).
