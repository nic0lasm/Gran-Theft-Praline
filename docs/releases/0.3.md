# Version 0.3 "Behind the wheel"

*Released ⟨date⟩ · macOS, Apple Silicon · [Version française](0.3.fr.md)*

Rue de la République now leads somewhere: get into a car, drive down to the
quai du Rhône and cross the pont Lafayette, among other cars driven by the
people of Lyon.

## New

- **Driving.** Renault Clio V with Chaos physics, damage by zone (bumpers,
  doors, bonnet) and a procedural three-cylinder engine sound that follows
  the revs and the throttle. A small panel shows the speed, the gear and the
  state of the car.
- **Traffic.** Cars driven by non-player characters follow the real streets
  (IGN BD TOPO, lanes set on the carriageway measured from the Grand Lyon
  kerbs), give way at junctions one at a time and brake behind each other.
  Three cars at once on an M1 Pro, which keeps the frame rate.
- **Carjacking.** Walk up to a car that has slowed down (under 12 km/h) and
  press **F**: the driver is pulled out and runs away.
- **A bigger map.** The quai du Rhône and the pont Lafayette, with its deck,
  join rue de la République.
- **Falling into the Rhône.** On foot you swim for a few seconds, then come
  back on the nearest embankment; a car sinks and you are brought ashore.

## Performance

Measured by the game itself on a MacBook Pro M1 Pro, "M1" profile, 300 s
drive: frame time p50 ⟨p50⟩ ms, p95 ⟨p95⟩ ms, ⟨share⟩ % of frames under
33 ms (about 30 fps).

## Controls

Same as 0.2; in a car: **Z / S** (W / S in QWERTY) accelerate and brake,
**Q / D** steer, **Space** handbrake, **F** get out (at speed you jump out).

## Known issues

- No traffic lights, pedestrians or police yet: they come with 0.4 and 0.5.
- Cars cut some junction corners and can touch a kerb there.
- Plane trees still have no leaves; some shop signs have no logo yet.

## Open it the first time

The app is not signed by Apple yet. Unzip it (if the download is in several
parts, put them in one folder and run `cat GranTheftPraline-0.3-macOS.zip.part-* > GranTheftPraline-0.3-macOS.zip`
first), then **right-click the app → Open → Open**. If macOS still refuses:
System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
