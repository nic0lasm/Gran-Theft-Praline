# Version 0.3.0 "Behind the wheel", with preview systems

*Released 10 November 2026 · macOS, Apple Silicon · [Version française](0.3.fr.md)*

Rue de la République now leads somewhere: get in a car, drive down to the
Rhône quay and cross the Lafayette bridge among other cars. This version
also brings, as previews, the systems planned for versions 0.4 to 0.6. They
are playable but not finished.

## What's new

- **Driving.** A Renault Clio V with Chaos physics and damage by zone
  (bumpers, doors, bonnet). A small panel shows speed, gear and the car's
  condition.
- **Traffic.** Cars driven by non-player characters follow the real streets
  (IGN BD TOPO). Their lanes sit on the carriageway measured from the
  Grand Lyon kerbs. They take junctions one at a time and brake behind the
  car in front.
- **Carjacking.** Walk up to a car that has slowed down (under 12 km/h) and
  press **F**: the driver is pulled out and runs away.
- **A bigger map.** The Rhône quay and the Lafayette bridge, with its deck,
  join Rue de la République.
- **Falling into the Rhône.** On foot you swim for a few seconds, then climb
  back onto the nearest bank. A car sinks, and you are brought back ashore.

## Preview (versions 0.4 to 0.6)

| System | State in this version |

| Pedestrians who walk, cross, get scared and run away | on |

| Police: wanted stars, chasing cars, sirens | on |
| Mini-map at the bottom left and full-screen map (**M**) | on |
| Saved settings menu (**Esc**): picture, sound, controls | on |
| Day/night cycle and weather (clouds, rain, fog on the Rhône) | on |

A system marked "off by default" is in the game but switched off, because it
went over the M1 frame budget or was not safe enough for this version.

## Performance

Measured by the game on a MacBook Pro M1 Pro, "M1" profile, over 330 s of
driving with the default settings and every system on: frame time p50
33.9 ms, p95 44.5 ms and p99 48.8 ms. 41.5 % of frames are under 33 ms
(about 30 fps). The graphics
processor is the limit. There was a single hitch over 100 ms, during
loading. The p95 is above our 42 ms target, and the next version will look
for those milliseconds.

## Controls

As in 0.2. In a car: **W / S** to accelerate and brake (Z / S on AZERTY),
**A / D** to steer (Q / D), **Space** for the handbrake, **F** to get out
(you jump while moving). **M** opens the map and **Esc** the menu.

## Known issues

- Cars cut some corners at junctions and can touch a kerb there.
- The preview systems are tuned tightly for the M1: few pedestrians and one
  or two police cars at most.
- The plane trees have no leaves yet, shop windows are opaque and the small
  street details (dirt, objects left around) are left out to keep within
  the frame budget. The Louis XIV statue and the fountains have no volume
  yet.
- Sound volumes by category cannot be set in the configuration file yet:
  the game uses its defaults.

## Opening the game the first time

The app is not signed by Apple yet. Unzip it. If the download comes in
several parts, put them in one folder and first run
`cat GranTheftPraline-0.3.0-macOS.zip.part-* > GranTheftPraline-0.3.0-macOS.zip`.
Then **right-click the app → Open → Open**. If macOS still refuses, go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".
