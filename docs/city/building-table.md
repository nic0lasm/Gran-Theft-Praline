# Building table of Lyon (added 2026-11-13)

One row per real building, from the République slice (Bellecour → Jacobins) out to the horizon
(2.5 km around the street axis), with what any building generator needs: our facade kit, PCG
building primitives (City Sample's `Assign_CitySample_Buildings` reads a footprint spline and
`@data.MaxHeight`), or an LLM driving the editor through Unreal MCP.

```
python3 tools/city/far_decor.py fetch --name presqu-ile        # CityGML zips (2-4 GB each), once
python3 tools/city/far_decor.py prep  --name presqu-ile        # -> citygml/cache, the zips can go
python3 tools/city/building_table.py fetch --name presqu-ile   # BDNB export of the Rhône (560 MB), once
python3 tools/city/building_table.py build --name presqu-ile --focus rue-de-la-republique
```

Output (out of Git): `assets/geo/processed/<zone>/buildings_lyon/`
`buildings_lyon.geojson` (geometry = footprint), `buildings_lyon.csv` (same fields plus
`footprint_wkt`), `summary.json` (counts per roof type, style, source).

## Coordinates

Local metres, x east, y north, from the zone origin in Lambert-93 (EPSG:2154, in the file's
`metadata`); heights in metres above the origin altitude (NGF-IGN69). Unreal world of the street
map: X = x·100, Y = −y·100, Z = z·100 (cm). The GeoJSON is therefore not in WGS84: read the
`metadata` block.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning |
| ---- | ---- |
| `id` | CityGML id (cadastral for plain buildings, UUID for remarkable ones) or BD TOPO id |
| `source` | `maquette-2018` (Métropole's 3D model) or `bdtopo` (zone building the model lacks) |
| `arrondissement`, `remarkable` | zip it comes from; remarkable building (basilica, churches...: keep its mesh) |
| `centroid_x_m`, `centroid_y_m`, `dist_axis_m`, `footprint_m2` | position, distance to the rue de la République axis, footprint area |
| `ground_z_m`, `eave_z_m`, `ridge_z_m` | lowest point, eave (median of the lowest point of each roof face, weighted by area), highest point (2018 model) |
| `height_eave_m`, `height_ridge_m` | wall height to the eave (use for `MaxHeight`) and total height |
| `roof_type`, `roof_pitch_deg` | `flat`, `pitched`, `mansard`, `mixed`, `unknown` from the slopes of the model's roof faces (BD TOPO rows: the street map's roof, else `unknown`); median pitch |
| `storeys`, `storeys_source` | storeys above ground: `carte-de-la-rue` (our generation), `bdnb`, `bdtopo` or `estime-hauteur` |
| `style`, `style_source` | `haussmann`, `ancien` (before 1853), `xixe` (1853-1914 off the Haussmann streets), `entre-deux-guerres`, `moderne`, `monument`, `inconnu`; from the street map, the BDNB year, or a flat roof |
| `year_built`, `era` | BDNB year of construction and its period (`avant-1800` ... `depuis-1976`) |
| `use`, `wall_material`, `roof_material` | BDNB (FFO) or BD TOPO |
| `ground_floor_commercial`, `ground_floor_shops`, `shop_names` | OpenStreetMap shops within 3 m of the footprint (zone only, empty beyond) |
| `in_slice`, `in_street_map`, `street_map_roof` | inside the slice clip; already generated by the street map, with its roof kind |
| `bdtopo_id`, `bdnb_id` | links to BD TOPO and BDNB |

## Measured (2026-11-13, 7 arrondissements)

13 316 rows: 13 095 from the 2018 model, 221 from BD TOPO; 13 132 linked to the BDNB, 12 204
with a year. Roofs: 10 151 pitched, 1 914 flat, 856 mixed, 179 mansard, 216 unknown. Slice:
229 rows, 172 with shops on the ground floor, 212 with a year, no two footprints overlapping.
Against the LiDAR of the street map (178 shared buildings) the ridge is 0.07 m off and the eave
0.7 m (median absolute differences).

One footprint is one building: the BATI copy of a remarkable building is dropped (the remarkable
row keeps it, 74 copies), a BD TOPO building is left out when the model's footprints together
cover half of it, and otherwise keeps only what they leave of it (ground from the zone's LiDAR).

## PCG footprints and the Lyon facade grammar

`tools/city/pcg_footprints.py --block <id> | --block-at x,y | --slice | --bbox` writes the chosen
buildings as closed rings in Unreal cm with `max_height_cm` (= `@data.MaxHeight`) to
`assets/geo/processed/<zone>/pcg/<name>.json` and `.csv`; a block is a set of touching
footprints. `tools/city/unreal/create_footprint_splines.py` (GTP_PCG_FOOTPRINTS) places one actor
with a closed spline per building, tagged `MaxHeight:<cm>`, `BuildingId:`, `Family:`, `Storeys:`,
`Roof:`, `Shops:`. `tools/city/pcg_lyon.py` proposes the Lyon grammar for City Sample's PCG
building primitives in Epic's Shape Grammar syntax: a family per style (haussmann, lyonnais,
moderne; remarkable buildings keep their 2018 mesh), a storey plan that fits MaxHeight with the
storey heights of `procgen.TYPOLOGY`, and one row grammar per storey role on the meshes of our
facade kit (`/Game/Environment/Facades/LyonKit`), one variant per building. Not yet tried in an
editor.

## Sources and limits

- Métropole de Lyon, textured 3D model 2018 (CityGML LOD2), Licence Ouverte v2: footprints,
  heights, roofs. Buildings built since 2018 are missing from it; inside the zone they come
  from BD TOPO (`source = bdtopo`, heights from BD TOPO, no roof).
- BDNB open (CSTB), millésime 2026-02-b, Licence Ouverte Etalab 2.0: year, storeys, materials,
  use. The owner tables of the export are never read.
- BD TOPO and LiDAR HD (IGN), OpenStreetMap (ODbL) for the shops, our street map for the slice.
- Footprints are the union of the model's roof and ground faces seen from above; courtyards
  inside a building stay as holes in the GeoJSON polygon (a spline takes the outer ring).
- Bridges are left out (not buildings). The remarkable buildings are rows like the others but
  are better kept as meshes (far decor).
